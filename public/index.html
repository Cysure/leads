How leads are scored

  • Sources: every lead file is merged on website domain, so each company appears once. The most senior contact is shown first.
  • Website reading: Crawlee and Cheerio fetch each homepage, robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt the way Google's crawler and AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) see them. Crawlee and Playwright then render it in Chromium on a phone screen for JS-built content, mobile layout and load time.
  • SEO score (0–100): points lost for each issue: HTTPS, title, description, H1, indexability, mobile viewport and overflow, sitemap, canonical, content depth, alt text, internal links, speed, freshness and outdated builds.
  • GEO score (0–100): whether AI assistants can read, trust and cite the company. Checks: AI crawler blocks, schema.org business data, content readable without JavaScript, address and phone on the page, about page, FAQ, linked profiles, llms.txt, Google Maps profile and reviews.
  • Visibility = 55 % SEO + 45 % GEO. Below the threshold, a lead counts as not visible. It is a rule-based on-site estimate, not a measured Google ranking position.
  • Google reviews: Playwright searches Google Maps for company + city and reads the rating and review count. A listing counts only if its website is the lead's domain or the name matches.
  • Deal score = 50 % visibility gap + 38 % fit (decision-maker, direct line, 5–80 staff, hiring, CH) + review strength + a bonus when a relaunch is needed.
  • Conversion score (0–100): the browser finds every call-to-action on the phone and desktop homepage. It checks: a CTA on the first screen, real button vs text link, colour contrast, tap size, tap-to-call, button wording, enquiry form length, sticky mobile CTA, cookie-banner cover, booking/chat/WhatsApp. Each finding comes with the fix. Green and red boxes on the screenshot mark strong and weak CTAs.
  • Revenue: the D&B figure where the lead has one, converted to CHF. Otherwise staff × the median revenue per employee of that industry, measured on the D&B leads. Wer-zu-Wem revenue bands are respected. With no size data, a typical SME is assumed and marked as a rough guess.
  • Close forecast: every lead is emailed. P(close by email) = reply rate × meetings per reply × win rate, scaled by visibility gap, seniority, personal vs info@ address and budget. Tier A/B leads are also called: reach within N attempts × meeting × win. The two channels combine as independent tries. Deal value is the average website price. Totals are the sum of the individual probabilities, with a 90 % band.
  • Tiers: A = call first (score ≥ 62 and reachable by phone), B = call second (≥ 52), C = email only, Visible = already doing well, skip.
